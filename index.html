The Namaste Zen Journey

At Namaste Zen, we believe that true wellness emerges from the harmony of mind, body, and spirit. Our mindful approach to yoga integrates ancient wisdom with modern wellness practices, creating a sanctuary where you can reconnect with your authentic self.

Nestled within the serene landscape of Scottsdale's most luxurious resort, our studio draws inspiration from the natural beauty of the Sonoran Desert, infusing our practice with its grounding energy and timeless tranquility.

"Yoga is not about touching your toes; it's about what you learn on the way down. At Namaste Zen, we create the space for that profound journey of self-discovery."

— Maya Reynolds, Founder

Class Experiences

Our intimate classes are limited to just 12 practitioners, ensuring personalized attention and a deeply immersive experience. Each class is thoughtfully designed to nurture your practice wherever you are on your yoga journey.

Desert Mindful Flow

A gentle yet powerful practice that synchronizes breath with movement, fostering mindfulness and inner peace inspired by the serene desert landscape.

Duration: 75 minutes

Sunrise Vinyasa

Greet the day with an energizing practice that awakens body and mind. Experience the magic of morning light streaming through our floor-to-ceiling windows.

Duration: 60 minutes

Moonlight Meditation

A deeply restorative evening practice combining gentle movement, breathwork, and guided meditation under the glow of moonlight in our garden studio.

Duration: 90 minutes
